Keep it in your pocket.

Tap + to choose images. Tap a card to open it, swipe left or right to browse, and pull down to close. Cards spring back when you let go of a short drag.

On iPhone, open the hosted page in Safari, choose Share → Add to Home Screen, and enable Open as Web App if shown. Open the new icon while online and wait for Ready offline. Import your images there, then test in Airplane Mode.

Images stay in this browser on this device. Nothing you import is uploaded. Keep your original images: clearing website data, removing the app, or storage eviction can remove this copy.
